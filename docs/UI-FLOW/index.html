1. Navigation map

Click a node to jump to its section. Solid lines are links; dashed lines are redirects (enrol, and back to / after a create or delete). Sub-pages (/dns/zones/{zid}, /scan/runs/{id}, WAF rules) sit under their parent. Sign-in rules: password with TOTP goes via /login/totp; REQUIRE_2FA without a factor goes to the enrolment page. The top bar (every signed-in page) holds the 12 working pages; the Menu holds account and system pages.
