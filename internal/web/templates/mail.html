Mail

The Postmark bridge per domain: inbound mail arrives at Postmark, the webhook below delivers the raw message over LMTP to your mail server, bounces come back as reports. ServanaGo provisioned the Postmark server, the domain and the DNS rows; it holds no mailboxes itself.

{{if not .Data.Ready}}
The mail bridge needs the Cloudflare DNS feature (Settings and the DNS token) and SERVANAGO_HOST set, so Postmark can reach a public webhook URL. {{helplink "mail"}}
{{else}} {{if not .Data.TokenSet}}
No Postmark account token yet — save it under Settings, Mail bridge. {{helplink "mail"}}
{{end}} {{if not .Data.LMTPSet}}
No LMTP target yet — set the host:port of your mail server's LMTP listener under Settings, Mail bridge. {{helplink "mail"}}
{{end}}
